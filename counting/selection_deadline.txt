Epoch choice is fixed to 22:00 Europe/Paris on 14 Nov 2026.
If all 30 epoch files exist before that, the choice runs then.
The hour is not moved after seeing a validation score or a loss curve.
Only epoch files whose size was stable before training is stopped are eligible.
Among those, the candidates are even indexes at or after 10.
If none exist, the even indexes that do exist are used.
Each candidate is predicted once at confidence 0.05 and cached.
The cutoff sweep reads that cache and does not predict again.
Kitchen is not in the choice or in the global total.
